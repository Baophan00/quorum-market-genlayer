How the outcome is decided

Validators read all three sources in one round and each reports a vote per source. Two or more agreeing votes settle the market; anything else voids it and refunds every buyer. The payout is arithmetic over the pool, so the last word is never the loudest person in the chat.

Buying costs credits, paying 1 credit per share. A winning share takes pool ÷ winning shares, and the cents that cannot be divided go one each to the earliest buyers.
